About me

I trained as a mechanical engineer and built my career in developing people. Over 19+ years I have moved from engineering recruitment to organisation development, and then to building learning and capability functions across engineering, EPC and manufacturing, in six countries.

I am SPHRi certified, and I keep returning to one question: how do we turn learning into business performance?

How I work

  • Start with the business problem, not the course catalogue.
  • Build with leaders, because managers decide whether learning is used.
  • Measure what matters, and report it in business terms.

Opportunities I welcome

  • Head of Learning and Development roles
  • Organisation development leadership
  • Talent management and HR transformation
  • Capability-building consulting
  • Speaking and facilitation

From talent acquisition to enterprise capability leadership

I began in engineering recruitment, then designed induction and onboarding programmes for graduate engineers, moved into organisation development consulting, and went on to build learning functions, a performance system and capability programmes across several industries. Starting in recruitment gave me a view of the whole employee lifecycle: how people are hired, inducted, developed, assessed and retained. That is why I treat learning as part of a larger talent and organisation system, not as a standalone training calendar. Along the way I recruited 2,000+ professionals, including at Adani Power and IRM Group and through consulting assignments.
